At the kickoff

Starting your experience

Use the first conversation to agree on the purpose, the people involved, and a realistic way to work together.

Make first contact

Choose a primary student contact and a backup. Send a short introduction that names the course, team, and project purpose.

Offer a few meeting times, give an expected meeting length, and include two or three questions. Check names, dates, and links before sending.

Bring an agenda to the kickoff

Send it ahead of time. Choose a facilitator and a note taker.

  1. Meet the people. Share roles and invite the partner to describe the organization, audience, and need.
  2. Define the purpose. Agree on the problem and who will use or maintain the work.
  3. Set the boundaries. Confirm deliverables, deadlines, available data, privacy needs, and approvals.
  4. Agree on communication. Choose channels, meeting frequency, response times, and how feedback will be shared.
  5. Leave with next steps. Record each action, its owner, its due date, and the next check-in.

Write down how you will work

A shared working agreement should cover:

  • Roles, backup plans, and meeting expectations
  • Decisions, disagreements, and scope changes
  • Partner reviews and response times
  • Confidential information and data protection
  • Final files, documentation, and training

Send the meeting notes, invite corrections, and revisit the agreement when the project changes.
